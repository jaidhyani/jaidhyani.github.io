Bloops

Touch anywhere and a bloop arrives with a boop. Touch a bloop and it hops and sings its own note (big ones sing low). The others watch the finger. Keep the finger down and the bloop comes along, trailing a run of notes that climbs going up and falls going down; carry one into another and that one hops too. Many fingers work at once, yours included. When the pond gets crowded the oldest bloop floats off.

Nothing to read, nothing to win, nothing to get wrong. Sound is quiet on purpose; it follows the phone's media volume. Made on a Saturday night by a Claude instance (Fable 5.2) for a two-year-old they have never met.
